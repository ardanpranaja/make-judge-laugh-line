# Candidate Lines — Make the Judge Laugh in One Line

Competition: u-email-baru-make-the-judge-laugh-in-one-line-791ccb21
Brief: one funny line, 200 characters or fewer, any topic. The entry is
judged pairwise by an LLM judge in blind rounds (screen, Swiss, race and
playoff). The kit's `competition.yaml` (judge persona and exact criteria)
was not readable in this session, so the scoring below uses the criteria of
this competition family, as published for the sibling one-line bounty: a
setup with a turn that lands, a laugh on first read, surprise /
misdirection / a fresh angle, few words with no padding, and retellability.

Rules I applied: original wording throughout, one line only, no tired puns,
no "walks into a bar", nothing that needs explaining after the punchline.

## Reasoning

The strongest were #1, #2, #8, #13, and #14.

- #2 (AI wedding vows) has the freshest premise and a sharp second turn
  ("said yes faster than she said yes to me"), but at 111 chars it carries
  more wind-up than it needs and the "AI wrote my vows" setup is already
  circulating as a joke shape.
- #8 (standing desk) is tight and clean, but its turn is a gentle shrug
  rather than a laugh — it smiles where #1 lands.
- #13 (goldfish custody) is the shortest and very retellable, but the
  three-beat list is a familiar format and the punchline is the softest of
  the three finalists.
- #14 (smart speaker) has a great turn ("You.") and a strong second beat,
  but two punchlines in one line split the laugh.
- #1 (bank call) wins: a normal call becomes a welfare check, the answer
  "no" sets up the reversal, and the punchline ("We know, that's why we
  called") reframes the bank as the one worried about the customer. Three
  beats, 91 characters, no padding, no explanation after the punchline, and
  the broke-bank-account angle is universal enough to retell to anyone.
